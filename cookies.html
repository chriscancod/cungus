Cookie Policy

Last updated September 9, 2026

Short version: 2AM doesn't set any cookies. No advertising pixels, no analytics, no tracking of any kind. This page explains what the site does keep in your browser instead, and why it's different.

Cookies we set

None. Not one. There is no advertising network on this site, no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no session-recording tool, no A/B testing service, and no third-party marketing tag. Nothing on this site is trying to work out who you are or follow you somewhere else.

What we store instead

Cookies get sent to a server on every request. This site uses local storage and session storage instead — data that stays in your browser on your device and is never automatically transmitted anywhere. It's used for the parts of a shop that literally cannot work without remembering something:

KeyWhat it holdsHow long
2am_cart The items in your bag, so they're still there if you close the tab or move between pages. Until you check out or clear it
2am_consent Your answer to the notice at the bottom of the screen, so it only asks once. Until you clear site data
2am_last_email The email from your last order, used only to show you relevant pieces next visit. Until you clear site data
2am_order / 2am_decline Your order summary, passed from checkout to the confirmation or decline screen. Session only — gone when you close the tab

Third parties that see your visit

Three services are loaded from outside this site. None of them set a cookie here, but they do receive a request from your browser, which means they can see your IP address:

  • Google Fonts — the typefaces this site is set in are fetched from Google's font servers. No cookie, but Google receives your IP when the fonts load. This one is on the list to be self-hosted so it stops happening at all.
  • Square — the payment form. Square's own scripts load only when you reach the payment step, never on a normal page view, and Square's privacy policy governs what happens there. This is what lets 2AM take a card without ever touching your card number.
  • Printify — product photography is served from their image CDN, so they see the request for each image on pages showing products.

Analytics

There is currently no analytics on this site at all — no visitor counts, no heatmaps, nothing. If a privacy-respecting, aggregate-only analytics tool is added later, the notice at the bottom of the screen will ask first, and nothing optional will load unless you say yes. Declining will always be a real choice that actually blocks it, not a button that does nothing.

Managing it yourself

You can clear everything this site has stored at any time through your browser's "clear site data" or "clear cookies and site data" option for 2amcases.online. Doing that empties your bag and makes the consent notice appear again — nothing else is affected, because there's nothing else.

Browser settings that block cookies won't break this site, because it isn't using any. Blocking local storage entirely will stop the bag from remembering items between pages.

Changes

If anything on this page stops being true — particularly if analytics gets added — the date at the top changes and this page gets updated in the same breath.
