Veynor Pet Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Mambru Inc. makes Veynor Pet for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy covers version 1.0 of the app.

What the app collects

Veynor Pet does not collect personal data from the app. It has no account, ads, analytics, or tracking.

What stays on your devices

The app saves your pet’s state and recent chat history on your devices. If you use both the iPhone and Apple Watch apps, they sync pet information between your paired devices through Apple’s WatchConnectivity.

Use Clear chat in the iPhone app to erase your chat history. To remove app data from both devices, delete the app from both your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Optional dictation

If you choose to dictate a message, Apple’s system dictation handles your voice and may process it under Apple’s privacy practices. Veynor Pet receives the resulting text and does not receive your audio.

Contact

If you email support, we use the address and information you provide to respond to your request. For privacy questions, email support@2amcases.online.
