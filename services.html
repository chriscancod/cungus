— Everything In One Place

What We Do

2AM is a small streetwear label, not a warehouse. Every piece is printed after someone orders it, every order carries an app activation code, and every email lands with one person. Here's the whole thing, plainly.

01

The Collection

Oversized, heavyweight pieces cut for cold-city wear — tees, hoodies, caps and the rest of the line as it grows. Drops are small by design and a sold-out piece may not return. Browse the catalog →

02

Made To Order

Nothing is pre-printed. When you order, your piece goes into production with one of two partners — Printify for the core line, Tapstitch for premium cut-and-sew. Ships in 3–5 business days with tracking emailed the moment it leaves the printer.

03

WARDROBE Activation

Every item comes with a code that links it to your closet in the WARDROBE app — track wear, build outfits, see the collection in one place. Codes arrive by email the moment payment clears, and on your confirmation screen. How it works →

04

2AM Rewards

One point per dollar. Reward coupons unlock automatically at 100, 250, 500 and 1000 lifetime points — no card, no app signup, tied to the email you order with. Check your balance →

05

Defect Cover

If a piece arrives damaged, misprinted or simply wrong, you get a free replacement or a full refund within 30 days of delivery — and you don't post anything back. Read the policy →

06

Real Support

The chat widget handles order lookups; everything else reaches a person. Every email is read personally, usually answered the same day. Sizing questions before you buy are welcome and genuinely the most useful thing to ask.

— From Checkout To Doorstep

1

You order

Card details go straight from your browser to Square, encrypted. 2AM never sees a card number — only whether the payment cleared.

2

It gets made

The order goes to the print partner and your piece is produced. This is why sizing matters up front — there's no shelf to swap it from.

3

It ships

3–5 business days to leave the printer, plus carrier transit. Tracking is emailed automatically.

4

You activate it

Your WARDROBE codes are already in your inbox. Open the app, add the piece, and the points from that order are on your Rewards balance.

5

If anything's wrong

One email with a photo, within 30 days. Replacement or refund, your call, nothing to post back.

What 2AM doesn't do

Worth saying out loud, so nobody finds out at the wrong moment:

  • No international shipping. US addresses only for now.
  • No size swaps or change-of-mind returns. Made-to-order means there's nothing to restock. Defects and wrong items are fully covered — that distinction is explained on the Returns page.
  • No bulk, wholesale or custom-design orders as a standing offer. If you want to ask about one anyway, email — it's a conversation, not a product.
  • No in-store pickup. There's no physical storefront; this is the whole shop.

That's the whole thing.

No hidden tiers, no membership, no upsell. Just the collection and what happens after you order.
